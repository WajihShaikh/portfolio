SEO vs AEO vs GEO in 2026: How to Improve Your Website’s Visibility in AI Search

What these overlapping terms mean, what Google actually documents, and how to build useful content without chasing AI-search myths.

Written byWajih Shaikh

Senior WordPress Developer & Web Designer

In this articleSEO, AEO and GEOHow they compareGoogle AI OverviewsPractical checklistMistakes to avoid

Quick answer: SEO improves organic search discoverability. AEO focuses on presenting clear answers to questions. GEO is an emerging label for improving discoverability and representation in generative AI experiences. They overlap: the strongest strategy is technically sound, original, well-organised content that satisfies search intent. None guarantees a ranking or AI citation.

As people discover information through search engines and conversational AI, website owners are hearing terms such as Answer Engine Optimization and Generative Engine Optimization. Some vendors present them as replacements for SEO, but traditional search fundamentals remain important.

What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization improves a site’s organic search visibility. Effective SEO is not just keyword repetition; it involves matching user intent, publishing useful information and maintaining technical quality.

  • Search intent and relevant topics.
  • Helpful, original content.
  • Appropriate page titles and descriptions.
  • Internal links and intuitive navigation.
  • Crawlable HTML, canonical URLs and indexing.
  • Mobile usability and performance.
  • Credible, naturally earned references and links.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization describes techniques that make content easy for answer systems to understand and use. It is not a separate Google ranking switch. Answer-friendly writing uses direct answers, descriptive headings, accurate definitions, clear examples and follow-up detail.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization is an emerging term for making content useful and discoverable in systems that generate answers, summaries or recommendations. Practical steps include publishing original evidence, clear context, precise explanations, transparent sourcing and an authentic author identity.

No content template or structured-data property forces ChatGPT, Gemini or Google AI search to cite a page. Visibility varies by system, query, retrieval and product behaviour.

Build one useful content system rather than three competing versions of each page.

Does traditional SEO still matter for Google AI Overviews?

Yes. Google’s AI features guidance says there are no special technical requirements to appear as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode beyond eligibility for Google Search with a snippet. Existing Search fundamentals remain relevant.

A site with blocked pages, missing content, duplicate URLs or weak information quality will not improve simply by adding “GEO optimised” to its footer.

A practical SEO, AEO and GEO checklist

1. Research visitors’ actual questions

Different queries have different intent: learning, comparison, instruction, commercial research and service selection. Give each page a coherent purpose instead of forcing every keyword into every post.

2. Put the essential answer near the top

A short, accurate answer helps readers decide whether the article solves their problem. Then add details, examples and limitations.

3. Use a meaningful heading hierarchy

Use one H1 for the title and H2/H3 headings for real sections. Clear headings help screen-reader users and make long articles easier to navigate.

4. Publish evidence of real experience and expertise

Google’s helpful-content guidance discusses E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness and Trustworthiness. It is a quality-assessment framework, not one direct ranking factor.

For a developer, credible proof can include real code, architecture notes, GitHub repositories, reproducible tests, owned screenshots and accurately described project experience. Never invent results, awards or first-hand product tests.

5. Implement article structured data correctly

Appropriate Article or BlogPosting structured data can help systems understand the headline, author, date and publisher. Keep it consistent with visible content. It does not guarantee ranking changes or AI mentions.

FAQ rich results are generally restricted to eligible authoritative government and health websites. A developer portfolio should not rely on FAQPage schema for a special search appearance.

7. Make content technically accessible

Use crawlable HTML, canonical URLs, responsive layouts, sensible metadata and a functioning sitemap. Do not hide the main article behind client-only rendering.

8. Earn trust through original information

Original case studies, measured comparisons, process documentation and tested examples distinguish useful work from generic summaries. If you did not run a test, do not claim a win rate.

9. Keep volatile facts current

Model names, features and API pricing change rapidly. Revisit posts after important changes and update the modification date only when the content actually changes.

10. Measure realistic indicators

Use Google Search Console for impressions, clicks and queries. AI-referral and citation visibility remains less complete; not every generated answer creates a visit.

Common mistakes to avoid

Keyword stuffing. Repeating “SEO, AEO and GEO” in every sentence makes a page less useful.

Copying generic AI output. Unverified text can contain incorrect claims, circular explanations and invented statistics.

Publishing duplicate country pages. Near-identical US, UK and Canadian articles with swapped place names rarely add value.

Inventing backlinks or citations. Misleading references damage trust and do not improve the underlying information.

Treating schema as a shortcut. JSON-LD describes a page; it does not guarantee a featured snippet, AI Overview citation or top position.

FAQs: SEO, AEO and GEO

What’s the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO concerns organic search discovery and performance. GEO focuses on how content may be discovered or used in generative experiences. Their practical foundations overlap.

Is AEO replacing SEO?

No. Clear answers are valuable, but sites still need searchable, technically accessible and trustworthy content.

Can my developer portfolio appear in AI search answers?

It may, depending on the search product, question, available sources and relevance. There is no guaranteed opt-in or markup tag.

Does FAQ schema improve AI rankings?

There is no reliable basis for claiming this. Write useful FAQs for readers and apply structured data only when appropriate.

How long does SEO take to work?

Results vary based on site quality, competition, relevance and other factors. No honest professional can promise a specific ranking by a fixed date.

Final thoughts

SEO, AEO and GEO work best when they reinforce each other. Make the site technically reliable, answer questions clearly, publish original evidence and build a trustworthy content library.

A professional developer portfolio can compete by showing real engineering expertise, not by trying to trick search engines with excessive keywords.
