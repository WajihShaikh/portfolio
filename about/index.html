Design judgment. Technical ownership.

I see the interface.
And the system behind it.

I’m Wajih. I work across design, frontend development and the WordPress backend, so the handover is as considered as the homepage.

I started working with WordPress and frontend development in 2022. Since then, I have worked across freelance, agency and in-house environments on business websites, ecommerce, landing pages, custom themes, plugins, API integrations and performance improvements.

I am most useful when a project needs both visual judgment and technical ownership. I can translate a Figma design accurately, but I also ask whether the page hierarchy makes sense, whether the backend will remain manageable and whether the implementation creates avoidable performance or search problems.

What good work looks like to me

A good website is not the one with the most animation or the highest number of sections. It is the one that explains the offer, earns trust, supports the visitor’s task and remains stable for the people who manage it.
