Useful answers

Questions about WooCommerce development.

Can WooCommerce connect to external systems?

Often, yes. The exact approach depends on the API, authentication, data direction and frequency of updates. I review those details before recommending an integration.

Can you customize the WooCommerce checkout?

Yes, within the limits of the payment stack and the selected checkout architecture. Changes should reduce friction without introducing update risk.

Do you also improve existing stores?

Yes. I can work on product presentation, responsive layouts, template issues, performance and maintainability without replacing the full store.
