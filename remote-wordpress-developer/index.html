How remote projects run

A process built for clarity, not constant meetings.

You always know what is being worked on, what is waiting for feedback and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Scope the real problem

    We start with the current website, business goal, required functionality and the people who will manage it.

  2. 02

    Agree on delivery

    The scope, milestones, responsibilities and communication rhythm are written down before the build begins.

  3. 03

    Build and review

    I share working progress, test responsive behaviour and keep feedback tied to the agreed goal.

  4. 04

    Launch and hand over

    The final website is checked, documented and handed over with the access and guidance the project requires.
